## Part 3 — UMW 100N03A MOSFET ×4 (LCSC C668200, DigiKey AU, AliExpress), counterfeit risk, and the AON7410 alternative

### Takeaway
**Genuine UMW 100N03A sources:**
- **LCSC:** US$1.00 for the minimum of 5 (A$1.44), plus checkout shipping. Prices are not published; 2024 Australian data suggests about A$3 by the slow direct line or about A$28 by DHL.
- **DigiKey AU:** A$1.75 each, 1,850 in stock. Delivered within about 4 days, but only free from A$60; otherwise A$24.

AliExpress "UMW" 10-packs (AU$5.37–5.44) are cheap but unverifiable, so counterfeits are a real risk.

The AON7410 (used in one Flix build) is available and cheap. However, it is a DFN-3×3 part with RDS(on) rated only at 4.5 V and 10 V gate drive, so it is a fallback rather than an equal.

### Cited Findings
- **Flix MOSFET warning.** "UMW 100N03A or [analog]… Warning: don't use KIA 100N03A or other manufacturers, they might not work!" — [Flix README](https://github.com/okalachev/flix)
- **How the author chose it (Telegram post, 1 Aug 2024).**
  - Measured motor stall current is about 5 A.
  - Choose an N-channel part with Vgs(th) "around 1–2 V". On the Id–Vds curve at about 2.5 V gate, it should pass more than 5 A at 3.7 V.
  - It should have a body diode.
  - He picked "the most popular MOSFET at Chip i Dip: 100N03A".
  - Source: [Telegram @opensourcequadcopter/33](https://t.me/opensourcequadcopter/33)
