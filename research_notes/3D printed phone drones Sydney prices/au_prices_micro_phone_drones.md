# Australian prices for the phone-flown ESP32 micro drones (ESP-FLY, LiteWing, Flix, ESP-Drone sensors, batteries, chargers, spares), delivered to Sydney NSW 2000

All prices were observed on **5 October 2026** (Sydney time) unless stated otherwise. "A$" means Australian dollars. Australian retailers' prices include GST unless noted. USD prices are converted at **1 USD = 1.4411 AUD**.

## What exchange rate, GST rules and battery-shipping limits apply, and which stores could be checked?

### Takeaway
USD prices are converted at 1.4411. Overseas sellers may add 10% GST at checkout on orders up to A$1,000. Only Australian sellers (Phaser FPV, Core Electronics) were confirmed to ship LiPo batteries to Sydney, and both send them by road only. Several big Australian distributors (DigiKey AU, Mouser AU, element14 AU, Jaycar, Altronics, eBay AU) blocked automated price checks.

### Inferences
- **Seeed versus Pakronics.** Seeed direct (A$86.45 + up to A$8.65 GST + China-warehouse shipping) is cheaper than Pakronics (A$150.83) unless Seeed's shipping to Sydney exceeds about A$55. Pakronics' "ships in 7 to 10 days" with 91 units suggests it orders from Seeed on demand rather than holding stock in Melbourne.
- **Motors.** The MMW CL-0615-19 is a close electrical match to the kit motor: 19,000 Kv × 3.7 V ≈ 70,300 rpm against the kit's "70,000 RPM", with the same 6×15 mm can and 0.8 mm shaft. The kit motors are soldered, so the Micro-JST 1.25 plug would be cut off.
- **Batteries.**
  - The 5.5 g mylipo 205 mAh PH2.0 pack fits the 150–350 mAh window and the 3 g payload limit.
  - The 450 mAh CNHL packs (15 g) are larger than Seeed's 150–350 mAh guidance and likely too heavy.
  - The mylipo pack is LiHV (4.35 V full). On the XIAO's onboard charger it will simply charge to the XIAO's standard LiPo limit; a HexaCharger can charge it to 4.35 V.
- **Polarity.** Check polarity with a multimeter before plugging any third-party "PH2.0" pack into the ESP-FLY. Whoop-style PH2.0 batteries and JST-PH boards are not guaranteed to share polarity (Phaser's mylipo note and CircuitDigest's MX2.0 warning show connector conventions vary).
- **Frames.** The user can print replacement frames and covers on the P2S, from the free MakerWorld remix or the paid Cults3D STL per the earlier report. That removes the need for Seeed frame spares.
